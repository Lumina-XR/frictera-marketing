Research Information

How Frictera gathers, handles and uses research and market information.

Research purpose

Frictera conducts structured research to understand operational friction in industries such as property operations, recruitment, trades and professional services. The purpose is to identify patterns, validate assumptions and inform the design of operational intelligence and governed intervention offerings. Research is always conducted lawfully, transparently and with respect for the people and organisations involved.

Sources of information

Our research draws on:

  • Publicly available information published by companies, regulators and industry bodies.
  • Public directories, company registries and professional listings.
  • Direct conversations with research participants who have given informed consent.
  • Anonymised operational data supplied by clients under agreement.

Participant rights

If you take part in Frictera research, you have the right to:

  • Ask questions before agreeing to participate.
  • Decline to answer any question or withdraw at any time.
  • Request that your contributions be anonymised or excluded.
  • Request access to or deletion of your personal data.

Independence and integrity

Research findings are reported honestly and without selective omission. We disclose limitations in sample size, source reliability and confidence levels. Frictera does not pay for positive findings or accept editorial control from research sponsors.

Ethical boundaries

We do not use deception, false identities or coercive methods to obtain information. We do not contact individuals who have asked not to be contacted. Research involving identifiable people or sensitive business information is reviewed before publication or sharing.

Current research programmes

Our current active research programme is focused on property operations in Dubai, including brokerages and property management organisations. Findings are used to shape pilot offerings and are not shared publicly without explicit consent.
