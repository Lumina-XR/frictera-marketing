Property Operations

Turn property enquiries into reviewed actions in minutes, not hours.

Frictera helps letting and property-management teams classify enquiries, identify missing information, prepare appropriate responses and preserve evidence — while keeping the operator in control.

Intelligent intake

Enquiries are classified, validated and prioritised so operators know what to handle first.

Human-reviewed drafting

Every response is prepared as a draft for operator review. Nothing leaves your business without explicit approval.

Missing-information detection

Frictera spots what is missing and asks for it clearly, reducing back-and-forth and improving first-response quality.

Escalation signals

Potential emergency indicators are flagged for immediate human review.

Evidence trail

Every classification, recommendation, draft and decision is preserved for audit, review and continuous improvement.

Impact estimates

See potential time and handling impact based on your own volume and workflows — clearly labelled as estimates.
