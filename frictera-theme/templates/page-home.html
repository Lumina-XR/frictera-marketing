Economic consequence

Not every inefficiency deserves intervention.

Operational noise is everywhere. Frictera helps you distinguish friction that materially affects time, capacity, cost, exposure, or opportunity from work that can wait. Intervention should be justified — not automatic.

  • Signal
  • Consequence
  • Justified action

Automate only where automation earns its place. Automation is one possible intervention — not the default. Frictera compares ways to improve a workflow, keeps consequential decisions under human authority, and measures whether the change worked.

Consequential decisions stay with the operator. Recommendations can be prepared; authority to act remains explicit. Capability does not automatically mean permission.

Example workflow

Property operations: a concrete example of the model.

In letting and property management, friction often appears as slow enquiry handling, missing information, and unclear handoffs. Frictera classifies intake, surfaces gaps, prepares drafts for review, and preserves evidence — without replacing your existing systems or removing operator control.

Structured intake

Enquiries are classified, validated and prioritised so operators know what to handle first.

Human-reviewed drafting

Every response is prepared as a draft for operator review. Nothing leaves your business without explicit approval.

Missing-information detection

Frictera spots what is missing and asks for it clearly, reducing back-and-forth and improving first-response quality.

Escalation signals

Potential emergency indicators are flagged for immediate human review.

Evidence trail

Every classification, recommendation, draft and decision is preserved for audit, review and continuous improvement.

Impact estimates

See potential time and handling impact based on your own volume and workflows — clearly labelled as estimates.
