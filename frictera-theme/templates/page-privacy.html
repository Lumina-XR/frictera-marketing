Legal

Last updated: 14 August 2026

1. Introduction

Frictera Limited (“Frictera”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy notice explains how we collect, use, store and share personal data when you use our website at frictera.com (“Website”) and when you engage with our services.

2. Data we collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Identity and contact data: name, job title, company name, email address, phone number and postal address.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and version, time-zone setting, browser plug-in types, operating system and platform, and other technology on the devices you use to access the Website.
  • Usage data: information about how you use our Website and services.
  • Marketing and communications data: your preferences in receiving marketing from us and your communication preferences.

3. Operational data collected through our intake forms

When you submit a contact or research form on the Website, we collect information about your operation so we can review the workflow you describe. This includes:

  • Business context: your business type, team size, and role.
  • Operational friction: the workflow problems you select, such as slow response to enquiries, leads not reaching the right person, missing information, missed follow-ups, or handoff delays.
  • Desired outcomes: what you want to improve, such as faster first response, fewer missed enquiries, better lead routing, or less manual administration.
  • Language and international workflow: the languages your team and clients work in, the markets you serve, how translations are handled, how out-of-hours requests are managed, and how enquiries are routed across time zones or channels.
  • Systems and handoffs: the tools and channels you use (for example CRM, property portal, email, WhatsApp, spreadsheet, property-management software) and where handoffs between tools or teams cause friction.
  • Urgency and context: how soon you need attention and any additional details you choose to provide.

This information is process-level. We do not ask for, and you must not provide, tenant or customer names, financial records, contracts, passport details, visa details, or any special-category personal data such as health, religion, ethnicity, or political opinion.

We use language and market selections only to understand operational communication requirements. We do not use them to infer nationality, ethnicity, religion, or immigration status.

4. Engagement intent and consent

The intake forms also record what you would like from Frictera and what permissions you give us. Options include a friction review, operational assessment, short research conversation, product demonstration, or pilot discussion.

There are two separate pilot-related choices, both unchecked by default:

  • Pilot interest — you are interested in discussing a bounded Frictera pilot if the workflow appears suitable. This alone does not authorise us to contact you about a pilot.
  • Pilot contact opt-in — you give separate permission for us to contact you specifically about pilot availability. We will only contact you about pilots if both interest and this opt-in are selected.

If you request a call, we record that request. Calendar scheduling is not yet live, so if a call is appropriate we will contact you by email to arrange a convenient time.

Any future newsletter or general marketing consent will be requested separately and is not obtained through these intake forms.

5. How we use your data

We use your personal data only where the law allows us to. Most commonly, we will use your personal data to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide the services you request.
  • Review the operational workflow you describe and assess whether Frictera may be able to help.
  • Manage our relationship with you, including notifying you about changes to our terms or privacy notice.
  • Improve our Website, services and customer experience.
  • Deliver relevant marketing communications where you have opted in.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory and professional obligations.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share your data with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our business (such as hosting, email, analytics and customer relationship management services), and with professional advisers, regulators or law enforcement where required by law.

When live intake is enabled, form submissions will be forwarded over an authenticated server-to-server connection to the Frictera public inbound gateway, validated, stored in our database, and rate-limited via our infrastructure. No automatic CRM update, broker routing, email send, or calendar booking occurs.

7. Data security

We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used or accessed in an unauthorised way, altered or disclosed. Access to personal data is limited to those employees, agents, contractors and third parties who have a business need to know.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@frictera.com.

9. Cookies

Our Website uses essential cookies to operate and may use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

10. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this notice and to comply with legal, regulatory, or professional obligations. Specific retention periods for inbound submissions are subject to formal privacy and legal review.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy notice or our data practices, please contact us at privacy@frictera.com.
